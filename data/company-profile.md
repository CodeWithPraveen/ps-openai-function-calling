# Globomantics Company Profile

## Company overview

Globomantics is an enterprise software company founded in 2010. It builds software for businesses of all sizes, with a focus on enterprise resource planning (ERP) and customer relationship management (CRM).

## Products

### EnterprisePro

- An ERP suite with modules for finance, HR, inventory, and supply chain.
- Available in the cloud or on-premises.
- Used by more than 500 enterprise customers worldwide.

### CustomerConnect

- A CRM platform for lead management, sales pipeline tracking, and customer support ticketing.
- Includes analytics and reporting.
- Integrates with email, calendar, and other business tools.

### SupportDesk

- A help desk product with ticket management and a knowledge base.
- Includes a customer self-service portal.
- Monitors and reports on service level agreements (SLAs).

## Customer support

### Support team

- More than 50 support agents across offices in Austin, Dublin, and Singapore.
- Three tiers: Tier 1 for basic questions, Tier 2 for technical support, and Tier 3 for advanced technical issues.
- Average first response time: 2 hours.
- Customer satisfaction rating: 85%.

### Current challenges

1. More than 5,000 support tickets every month.
2. Product questions that need technical expertise.
3. Customers who expect support 24 hours a day.
4. Integration issues with third-party systems.
5. A knowledge base that is hard to keep up to date.

## Support hours and contact

- Tier 1 support is open 24 hours a day, Monday to Friday.
- Weekend support covers Priority 1 incidents only.
- Email: support@globomantics.com
